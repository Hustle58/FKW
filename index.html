What do the readings mean?

24-hr PSI is NEA's headline index, updated hourly from the past 24 hours of readings.

  • 0–50 Good
  • 51–100 Moderate
  • 101–200 Unhealthy
  • 201–300 Very unhealthy
  • 301+ Hazardous

1-hr PM2.5 (µg/m³) is NEA's hourly fine-particle reading, which reacts faster than the 24-hr PSI. NEA doesn't publish a separate "1-hr PSI", so this is the 1-hour figure shown. Bands: Normal 0–55, Elevated 56–150, High 151–250, Very high 251+.
